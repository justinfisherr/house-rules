# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
A friend or house-sitter staying in the apartment, plus Justin and Heeyeon as the authors. Read on a phone, often while standing in the room being described.

## Product Purpose
A single-page house guide: WiFi, two cats, feeding, litter, water, plants, trash, and parking. Success is the sitter finding the answer without asking.

## Positioning
A warm, specific handover note, not a rental manual: pet names, quirks, and real photos instead of generic policy.

## Operating Context
Read on a phone during a short stay. Information is stable; photos are real. No login, no backend.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static HTML on GitHub Pages with local photos. No build step. Content is authoritative and personal.

## Brand Commitments
Warm pink palette, friendly rounded type, real cat photos. Keep the voice human and specific.

## Evidence on Hand
Real photos of Clover, Gongju, and the apartment, and first-hand instructions.

## Product Principles
1. Answer the sitter's next question in one screen.
2. Be specific: names, colors, quantities.
3. Keep the tone warm, calm, and human.
4. Readable on a phone at arm's length.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Text at 4.5:1 or better on pink grounds; clear heading order; readable on a phone.
