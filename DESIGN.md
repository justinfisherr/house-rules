# Design System: House Guide

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Note on the Counter"**

A pink, friendly handover note with real photos and specific instructions. Softer and warmer than a rental manual, but still scannable while standing in the room.

## Colors

Cream ground, layered pinks, deep berry text. Pink is for structure and emphasis; body copy stays deep enough to read on every pink surface.

## Typography

Nunito throughout, heavy for headings and semibold for instructions.

## Layout

Single column with photo strips, a horizontal nav, and section cards. Inner groups are flat, separated by rules.

## Shapes

20px section cards, 12px controls, rounded images. No nested cards.

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do** keep instructions specific and readable, keep photos real, and keep heading order clean.
**Don't** nest cards or use thin low-contrast text on pink.
