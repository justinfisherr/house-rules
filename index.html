📋 How to Water Indoors
1
Bring plants into the bathroom
2
Use the watering container with handle
3
Add tap water + a little houseplant water conditioner (in the kitchen)
4
Pour until the soil is fully wet
5
Let them drain in the bathroom, then return to their spots
👆
Finger test: Stick your finger in the soil. Wet → skip it. Dry → water it.
